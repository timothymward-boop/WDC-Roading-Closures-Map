Reporting Requirements

If your life is at risk, call 111 right away.

Please only use this form to report issues that are impacting the function of the WDC Local Roading network (e.g. major slips, flooding, or fallen trees).

Note: This is for Whangarei District Council local roads only. For State Highway or NZTA issues, please contact Waka Kotahi (NZTA) directly.

For all other council-related issues, please use our Knowledge Base / Request System .

Urgent requests

Contact us on 0800 932 463 or 09 430 4200 (24 hours a day) if you have an urgent issue.

    Important information and disclaimers regarding this website

    Information Scope

    This dashboard provides a real-time overview of known road issues and status updates within the network. Please note: The issues listed are only the ones currently reported and verified. There may be other hazards or closures not yet reflected on this map.

    Safety Disclaimer

    Always stay safe and use general caution when traveling. Do not rely on this application as your sole source of truth for navigation or safety decisions. Conditions can change rapidly.

    NZTA Data Source

    Information sourced from NZTA is provided for general advice only. This data is verified through the NZTA Journey Planner. For critical travel decisions, please verify information directly through official NZTA channels.
